**Interface Sounds** by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain):

- "12 Indie Game Music Loops" (https://opengameart.org/content/12-music-loops): bgm_menu_title_1_relaxed, bgm_storytime,
