# Audio credits

Sound effects replaced in 2.0.9 (menu click, hover, block placed, invalid move, achievement) come from
**Interface Sounds** by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain):
https://opengameart.org/content/interface-sounds — click_001, select_002, drop_002, error_004, confirmation_002.

Music: the default music ("Classic" pack) is a playlist of songs by **Juhani Junkala** (SubspaceAudio,
juhanijunkala.com), CC0 1.0, from OpenGameArt:
- "Chiptune Adventures" (https://opengameart.org/content/4-chiptunes-adventure): Stage 1, Stage 2;
- "12 Indie Game Music Loops" (https://opengameart.org/content/12-music-loops): bgm_menu_title_1_relaxed, bgm_storytime,
  bgm_space_upbeat_F, Retro Sports bgm_stage_3 and bgm_stage_final, Super Action Chiptunes bgm_stage_6, 7, 9.
Files: assets/sounds/cc0/jj-*.mp3 (loudness-normalized, re-encoded). Each song plays to its end, then another one.
The other music packs and sound packs were generated for the game.
