## Services

Blockerino talks to Supabase (database, accounts), Discord (Activities and sign-in), Google (sign-in),
Expo / EAS (builds, updates, push delivery) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push on Android). They are used
under their own terms; none of their code or branding is part of the game.
